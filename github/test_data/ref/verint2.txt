The customer was trying to make a payment instruction on Telcomind Net with a USD account, but was having difficulty. The customer was unable to see the account in the payment instructions at agent2, and was getting an ER error message saying a signature requirement was not defined in the account. The agent was able to help the customer by explaining that the account was missing a signature limit or transaction limit, and that the system administrator would need to fill it out in order for the customer to make payments.
